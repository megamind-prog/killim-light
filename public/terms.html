Terms of use

Last updated: 22 October 2026

By using Killim Light you agree to these simple rules. They are here to keep the lights honest and the site friendly.

Using the map

  • Lights, prices and notes are set by the nakamal owners. We check every nakamal in person before it goes on the map, but we can't promise a light is right at every moment. If it looks wrong, tap "Is this light wrong?".
  • Killim Light is free and comes as it is. We are not responsible for a trip to a nakamal that turned out to be closed, or for anything that happens at a nakamal.
  • Please drink kava responsibly, and never drive after drinking.

Ratings and comments

  • Be fair and honest. One rating per phone per nakamal.
  • No insults, threats, personal details about anyone, or advertising. Our team removes comments and ratings that break these rules.

For nakamal owners

  • Our team may list nakamals it knows about before the owner joins. These show their usual hours only (no live light). If it's yours, tap "Run this nakamal? Claim it" on its page; to have a listing removed, contact us.
  • Only register a nakamal you own or run, or have the owner's permission to manage.
  • Keep your light honest: set it when you open, when kava runs low and when you close. A light that is often wrong may be hidden.
  • Only upload photos you took or may use. You keep ownership; you allow Killim Light to show them on your nakamal's page and through our open data, next to your listing.
  • Our team may visit, edit, hide or remove a listing that is wrong, unsafe or breaks these rules, and will try to call you first.
  • Specials and events must be real and happen as posted. No misleading prices, and nothing unsafe or against the law.
  • Keep your login to yourself. If you lose your phone or password, contact our team and we will help you get back in.

Open data and code

Nakamal listings are open data under CC BY 4.0, the website code is under the MIT licence, and the base map is © OpenStreetMap contributors. See the developers page for details.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms as Killim Light grows; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: hello@killimlightvanuatu.com.
