Privacy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Killim Light shows which nakamals in Vanuatu have kava ready. We collect as little as we can, we never sell anything, and we never show ads.

If you use the map

  • No account needed. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Kept on your phone only: your favourites, your language, the last lights (so the map works without signal), and a random code for your phone.
  • Ratings and "light is wrong" reports are stored with that random code, so each phone counts once. They are not linked to who you are. Written comments (when switched on) are public.
  • Page views: we count how many phones open each nakamal per day, so owners see how many people look. Only the count is kept.
  • "Alert me" notifications: if you turn one on, your browser gives us a notification address for that nakamal. We use it only to send that alert, and delete it when you turn the alert off or it stops working.
  • Your location ("Near me" and directions) stays on your phone. It is never sent to us.
  • "Alert me when kava is ready near me": only if you turn this on, we keep your place rounded to about 1 km, the distance you chose and a notification address, just to send those alerts. Turning it off deletes them.

If you run a nakamal

  • Your login: an email and password, a Google sign-in, or a phone number and PIN made by our team.
  • Seen only by our team: your name and phone number, so we can arrange the visit and help you. They are never shown publicly or given to anyone else, and the open data never contains them.
  • Public: what you put on your nakamal's page (name, place, light, note, kava, prices, hours, photos, specials and events) and the host name and picture you choose. The history of your light (when it was green, amber or closed) is public too, like the light itself. Other apps and websites may show this through our open data.
  • Your stats (page views, followers, nights open) are shown only to you and our team.
  • Activity log: the team page records who changed what (for example "set Salo to Kava ready") so we can fix mistakes. Entries are deleted after 90 days.

If you use our open data (developers)

  • A key is optional. If you get one, we keep your app name, your email and what you're building, a daily count of requests made with the key, and the webhook addresses you add. We store only a fingerprint of the key itself.
  • Our team can see this list and switch a key off if it's misused.

Where it is stored

The website, data and logins are hosted by Netlify, Inc. (servers outside Vanuatu). The page fonts load from Google Fonts. Map data comes from OpenStreetMap and is served from our own site. We use no advertising or tracking cookies; the only cookie keeps owners and the team logged in.

Removing your data, and questions

Owners can remove a nakamal themselves on the owner page. To delete your whole account and everything linked to it, or for any question about your data, email hello@killimlightvanuatu.com or tell our team on a visit.

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
